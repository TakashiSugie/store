Privacy Policy

Overview

SugieTechLab ("we", "us") is an independent developer based in Japan. This policy explains how we handle personal information and user data in all mobile apps and services we develop and distribute (our "Apps").

This policy applies to all of our Apps. Where an App handles data in its own way, we explain it in the App or on a separate page for that App, which takes precedence over this page for that App.

Information We Collect and How We Use It

Depending on its features, an App may collect and use the following information, only as far as needed.

Data you enter and your progress

Data you enter or create in an App, and your progress, scores and settings, are used to provide the App's features and to improve your experience.

Device features

Some Apps may use device features such as motion sensors, the camera, the microphone, or Health data. These are used only in Apps that have such features and only as needed for them. We always ask for your permission first through the system's permission settings.

Analytics and crash reports

To improve quality and stability, some Apps may use third-party services such as Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. This data is aggregated and analyzed in a form that does not identify you. Each App's page states whether it uses these services.

Storage and Transfer of Data

As a rule, data collected by our Apps is stored only on your device.

Some Apps may offer cloud sync or backup. In that case, data is sent over encrypted connections such as SSL/TLS, and data stored in the cloud is kept under appropriate access control.

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising

Some of our Apps use third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. To show ads, these services may use cookies (or similar technologies) and advertising identifiers (IDFA / AAID).

You can limit or turn off the use of advertising identifiers in your device's settings. For details, please see the privacy policy of each advertising service.

  • Legal basis: we handle data to provide the App you use (performance of a contract) and, for personalized ads, on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the App.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive a copy of it. Data kept only on your device can be deleted by deleting the App.
  • Data collected by advertising services may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own, including the United States, under the safeguards described in each service's privacy policy.
  • You have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address below.

Children

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
