Eki Mikke: Tokyo Train MapPrivacy Policy

About This Page

This page explains how the iPhone and iPad app "Eki Mikke: Tokyo Train Map" (the "App", 駅みっけ in Japanese), provided by SugieTechLab ("we", "us"), handles your data. Anything not covered here follows our general Privacy Policy.

Data the App Handles

  • Quiz records (questions asked and answered correctly for each station; the line, mode, score, time and date of each game; completed lines and when they were completed)
  • Settings (vibration, number of questions and order, your last game and the stations you missed, and how many free uses of other railways and subways you have used today, and on how many days you have opened station cards, which is used only to decide when to ask for an App Store rating)
  • Whether you have bought Premium (an in-app purchase managed by Apple)

The App does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or health data, and there is no account to create. The App itself does not include analytics or crash reporting.

The map and station data (based on hashcc/railmaps, CC0; Ekidata; and Wikidata, CC0) are included in the App. The App does not go online to search stations or make questions, and the map works offline.

Where Data Is Stored and How to Delete It

Your records and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers (we do not run any servers). They are not synced to iCloud. Deleting the App deletes your records and settings (scores already sent to Game Center, described below, remain).

When you share a result, the app you choose (such as a social network) receives an image of the result (line, mode, score, time and the number of stations answered on that line) and a short text with a link to the App Store. It does not include your name, account or location. You decide whether to share and which app to share with.

Game Center

The App offers leaderboards and achievements through Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the App sends the following to Apple when you finish a game:

  • Your time for each line (the total of your best times in the Name Quiz and the Map Quiz, plus 5 seconds for each wrong answer)
  • The number of stations you have answered
  • Your achievement progress (such as the number of completed lines and stations answered)

Leaderboards show your score with your Game Center nickname, and other players can see them. Your Game Center account is managed by Apple, and we do not receive it. For details, please see Apple's Privacy Policy. If you are not signed in to Game Center, nothing is sent, and the App works the same in every other way.

Advertising

The App shows Google AdMob ads on the result screen and a full-screen ad once every three games. When you choose to, it also shows an ad that, if you watch it to the end, lets you use all lines for the rest of the day (a rewarded ad). To serve ads, Google may collect information about your device and usage (such as your IP address, device information, and how you view and interact with ads). For details, please see the Google Privacy Policy.

The App does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency), so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.

Where the law requires consent, such as in the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before showing ads. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Ad Privacy Settings.

We never give your quiz records or settings to Google for advertising. If you buy Premium, the App does not load ads and sends no information for advertising.
