About This Page

This page explains how the iPhone and iPad app "Nippon Meibutsu Map" (the "App", ニッポン名物マップ in Japanese), provided by SugieTechLab ("we", "us"), handles your data. Anything not covered here follows our general Privacy Policy.

Data the App Handles

  • Your records (date, rating, notes and photos for each item)
  • Photos (taken with the camera, or chosen by you)
  • Entries you add yourself (name, prefecture, genre and notes)
  • Settings (genres you are interested in, whether you finished the first-run guide, a counter used to space out full-screen ads, and the minimum version remembered for the outdated-version check)
  • The purchase status of the in-app purchase that removes ads (managed by Apple)

The App does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or health data. There is no account registration. The App itself includes no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

The specialty and sight data and the blank map are included in the App. No network connection is needed to show them.

Storage and Deletion

Your records, photos, added entries and settings are stored only on your iPhone or iPad and are not sent to our servers (we have no server that receives them). They are not synced to iCloud.

You can delete records and photos one by one inside the App. Deleting the App removes all records, photos, added entries and settings from your device. Because we do not receive your records or photos, we cannot delete them for you.

Camera and Photos

  • Camera: used only to take a photo to attach to a record.
  • Photos: when you choose a photo, the App receives only the photos you select. It does not read your whole photo library.

The App does not use location, contacts or the microphone.

Outdated-Version Check

When the App starts and when it returns to the foreground, it reads a small file from a page we publish (GitHub Pages) that states the minimum supported version. It only reads the file; it sends none of your data or records. If the connection fails, the App keeps working. As with any network request, the host (GitHub) may log technical information such as your IP address.

Advertising

The App shows banner ads from Google AdMob at the bottom of the screen, and, once you have more than 10 records, a full-screen ad after every third new record you save. To serve ads, Google may collect information about your device and usage (such as IP address, device information, and ad views and interactions). For details, see the Google Privacy Policy.

The App asks for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). The device advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow it, to show ads that are more relevant to you. All features of the App are available even if you do not allow it. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device.

In regions where the law requires consent, a consent screen is shown before ads. You can change your choice in Settings → "Ad privacy settings" (shown only in those regions).

Your records, photos and notes are never passed to Google for advertising. If you buy the in-app purchase that removes ads, the App does not load ads or send any information for ads. Apple handles the purchase, and we do not receive your payment details.

Contact

In Settings → "Contact", the App opens an email draft. To help us investigate, the draft includes the App version, the OS version, the device model and whether ads were removed. It reaches us only if you review it and send it; nothing is sent automatically. The contents of your records, photos and notes are never included.

For other inquiries, please use the Support & Contact page.
